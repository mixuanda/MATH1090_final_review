\chapter*{使用说明}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{使用说明}

这份讲义的目标是成为期末复习用的 textbook-like review note。正文用中文解释，数学术语保留英文，例如 proposition, predicate, function, equivalence relation, Dedekind cut, supremum, Cauchy sequence, cardinality, group。每一道来自 Lecture Notes, worksheet, homework, and midterm 的题目都应当有独立题目条目、完整的 Solution，以及说明关键想法的 Explanation。

本讲义的证明写法遵循两个原则。第一，所有结论都要由 definition, axiom, theorem, or previously proved statement 推出，不使用跳步式断言。第二，Solution 负责给出形式化推导，Explanation 负责说明为什么要这样选择证明路线，特别是在 quotient construction, supremum, Dedekind cut, limit, and cardinality arguments 中说明关键难点。

\chapter{Final Review Checklist}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item Logic: 能够把 English statements 翻译成 propositional 或 predicate formulas，并用 truth table, direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction, and induction 完成证明。
  \item Sets and functions: 能够用 element chasing 证明 set equality, subset inclusion, image/preimage identities, injectivity, surjectivity, and bijectivity。
  \item Relations and quotient constructions: 能够验证 equivalence relation, partial order, equivalence class, and well-defined operation。
  \item Number systems: 能够从 Peano axioms 出发构造 \(\N,\Z,\Q\)，并解释 recursive definitions, representatives, and arithmetic laws。
  \item Real numbers: 能够使用 order, bound, supremum, Dedekind cut, decimal expansion, and Cauchy sequence 的定义完成证明。
  \item Limits: 能够将 \(\varepsilon\)-\(N\) and \(\varepsilon\)-\(\delta\) definitions 展开成完整的 inequality proof。
  \item Big sets and structure: 能够使用 bijection, injection, Cantor's theorem, Cantor-Bernstein theorem, Axiom of Choice, Zorn's Lemma, well-ordering, monoid, group, and homomorphism 组织证明。
\end{enumerate}
